## Ideas queue
- Isolated false D runs (~1.3 per game since v1, ~17% of false D frames): the ones that split possessions. Find their cause (tracking glitch past a line? long fill?) before adding rules.
- diagnose_dead.py: only ~41% of true dead stretches are touched; the rest are stops with the ball in play area (fouls, offsides) -> ball-still rule. Labels v1 count ~145 dead stretches per game (more than ~100 real stoppages): read with care.
- Quick free kicks (taker next to the ball, 1-3 s) are missed on purpose: indistinguishable from a player controlling the ball in open play.
- Edoardo's idea, to revisit: short D between two stretches of the same team in mid-pitch -> H. Fixes our false D but also deletes true quick-restart D (truth H D H = two possessions); needs team labels, so it belongs to postprocess.py (Pietro). Measure on external before proposing.
- Many correct dead runs are only 2-3 s (they vanish at MIN_DEAD_S 3 s): we see only a piece of the stoppage. Lengthening them (later restart, earlier start) beats deleting short runs.
- Labels v1 audit (audit_labels.py): 145 true D stretches per game; ~14 per game shorter than 2 s (gaps between phases, < 1% of D frames). Only ~15% of stops have the same team before and after: only those merge two possessions when missed.
- Merged versions at possession level (compare_versions.py): every step gains on both even and odd games. Rule 2 adds no swallowed possession. v1.2 (dead until restart) takes swallowed 19 -> 33, but 30 of 33 last < 5 s (20 < 2 s): mostly mini-possessions of the labels, all from rule 1.
- Missed same-team stops (diagnose_possessions.py): 157 of 463; 85 with the ball moving mid-pitch, 44 past or near a line, 14 ball still mid-pitch, 14 ball missing -> rule 2b (everyone walks).
- Kick-off after a goal: the other team kicks off, so that stop never merges two possessions: low value for spellF1.
- Edoardo's idea, next: a free kick is taken where the offence occurred (Law 13; no official tolerance, in practice a little) -> the restart counts only if the ball leaves from near where it was when the stop began; a pass back to the taker then does not end the dead run.
- Next for rule 2b: Edoardo's duel idea. A foul = an H and an A player both at the ball, one of them stops, then everyone walks within 1-2 s. Use it to start D at the duel, end it only when the ball leaves from near the duel spot, and allow WALK_MIN_S 6 s (F1 0.681) only after a duel. Tried as v2.2 on 2026-11-13: not kept (see table).
- Next (2026-11-13 evening plan, in order): (1) coverage inside touched stops: do we miss the start or the end of the stops we already see? then backdate / extend D; (2) kick-off shape after goals and at the start of halves (all players in their own half, ball on the centre spot); (3) outs the tracker does not see (ball vanishing near a line) with ball_v2; (4) rule 2b smoothing window and pass speed (not swept yet); (5) Smart Tagging set pieces as reinforcement only (cannot be measured: tags = [] on external games).
